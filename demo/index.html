Electrode signal, live

The strip's current falls as 1 over the square root of time. Only a real reading draws this curve.  

No colour to judge

The reader measures current, so lighting, eyesight and colour charts stop mattering.

pH does not fool it

A dried buffer pad on the strip holds the sample near pH 5.5, where almost all free chlorine is HOCl.

One strip, one record

Each strip has a serial and works once. The reader signs the full curve with place and time.
